# Conversation Log

> This file is the *timeline* of your OpenCode conversations.
>
> It is read at the start of every session (so the agent knows what you worked
> on before) and it grows silently in the background: each meaningful exchange
> is appended as a short, dated summary — never deleted, never rewritten.

## How entries are written

```
- YYYY-MM-DD <short summary of what was decided / changed / done>
```

Example:

```
- 2026-09-12 Installed the Wakanda4Ever Omarchy plugin & enabled OpenCode
   persistence (user-memory + conversation-log).
```

That's it — one bullet per session. Over time it becomes a neat story of
everything you and your agent built together :)
